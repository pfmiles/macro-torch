<objective>
Fix a bug in `catLeveling()` where OOC (Omen of Clarity) procs with 5 combo points
result in no skill being cast.

Purpose: When OOC is active and combo points are at 5, the M8 Builder module's outer guard
(`comboPoints < 5`) blocks entry entirely, while M5-M7 (Rip, Rake, Bite) are all guarded
by `not ooc`. The result is a dead zone: no module executes, wasting the OOC proc.
Output: Modified `classes/druid/leveling.lua` with the M8 outer guard expanded to also
allow entry when OOC is active.
</objective>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task: Fix M8 Builder outer guard to allow OOC entry at 5 CP</name>
  <files>classes/druid/leveling.lua</files>
  <action>
    In `catLeveling()`, line 180, change:
      `if macroTorch.isFightStarted(clickContext) and clickContext.comboPoints < 5 then`
    to:
      `if macroTorch.isFightStarted(clickContext) and (clickContext.comboPoints < 5 or clickContext.ooc) then`

    This allows the M8 Builder module to activate when OOC is active even at 5 CP.
    The OOC branch (lines 199-209) already uses `shred('ready')` / `claw('ready')`
    with no energy check — the inner logic is correct and needs no change.

    Rationale:
    - M5 Rip: guarded by `not ooc` — correct, don't waste OOC on a finisher
    - M6 Rake: guarded by `not ooc` — correct, don't waste OOC on a refresh
    - M7 Bite: guarded by `not ooc` — correct, don't waste OOC on a finisher
    - M8 Builder: was guarded by `comboPoints < 5` only — BUG: blocks OOC at 5 CP
    - M9 FF: guarded by `not ooc or not inCombat` — correct, FF is lowest priority
    - Fix: expand M8 guard to `comboPoints < 5 or ooc` — now OOC at 5 CP enters the
      builder, uses the free cast on Shred/Claw, and flows through normally

    This change only affects `catLeveling()`. `catAtk()` in `classes/druid/combo.lua`
    is not affected (it uses a different module structure).
  </action>
  <verify>
    <automated>grep -n "comboPoints < 5" classes/druid/leveling.lua</automated>
  </verify>
  <done>
    Line 180 reads `isFightStarted(clickContext) and (clickContext.comboPoints < 5 or clickContext.ooc)`.
    The OOC + 5 CP dead zone is eliminated: OOC procs at 5 CP now correctly trigger
    a free Shred or Claw through the M8 Builder module.
  </done>
